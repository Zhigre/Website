Welcome to my living portfolio. This site is where I collect cyber projects, OSINT writeups, scripts, scam notes, wiki work, and anything else that helps show the work behind the learning.

The site now builds from simple content files. Edit the Markdown files in `content/pages` for normal pages, or add a new file to `content/posts` when you want a new article to appear on the Articles page.
