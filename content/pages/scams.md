I hate scams.

There are people who indiscriminately target vulnerable people to make a quick buck. To do my bit, I record scams that are sent my way and broadcast warnings for people who may be in the firing line of the same campaigns.

I post warnings to X at [@Zhigre_X](https://x.com/Zhigre_X), usually with a screenshot and a short explanation.
